The Bakery Story

North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen with a simple goal: to bake bread that brings people together. Years later, we opened our doors to the community. We believe in the power of breaking bread, sharing stories, and treating every customer like a neighbor.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We source 100% of our organic wheat flour from local mills within a 50-mile radius. By partnering with local farmers for our dairy, fruits, and eggs, we ensure that every bite supports our local economy.
